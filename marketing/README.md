## Rules these ads follow, and why

- **Special Ad Category.** Run the consumer ads under Meta's **Financial products and services** special ad category. It limits targeting by age, gender and zip code; skipping it gets ads rejected or the account restricted.
- **No personal attributes.** Meta rejects ads that say or imply something about the viewer's own finances, such as "your bad credit" or "Are you in debt?" These ads talk about the activity and the product instead.
- **No results promises.** Nothing claims removals, points or approvals. Screens are labelled as sample data, and no screen shows a score increase.
- **Price stated.** The consumer ads say "Free to start" and the $37 together, so nobody feels baited.
- **Operator earnings.** The operator ads say "earn a share" with no dollar figure or percentage. A specific number is an earnings claim the FTC treats strictly. Only publish a rate once it's in your operator agreement.
