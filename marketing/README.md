## Tax-pro demo ad (32 s): voiceover to the timecodes

Point the ad at `https://leverageiq100.com/demo?utm_source=facebook&utm_medium=paid&utm_campaign=taxpro_demo&utm_content=motion`. No prices in the ad: the demo video sells the offer.

| Time | On screen | Voiceover |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00–0:04 | "You do their taxes." | You do their taxes. So why is someone else getting paid to fix their credit? |
| 0:04–0:08 | Client dashboard | Your client uploads their three-bureau credit report. |
| 0:08–0:12 | Audit findings | Leverage IQ finds the errors across all three bureaus. |
| 0:12–0:17 | Dispute letter | It writes law-based dispute letters, round by round. |
| 0:17–0:22 | Branded client view | All under your logo, with compliance built in. |
| 0:22–0:26 | Funding pipeline | Then it moves them toward business funding, with revenue share for you. |
| 0:26–0:32 | End card | Watch the full demo. Tap the link below. |

About 70 words: a calm, confident read fits without rushing.

**Ad copy.** Primary text: "Add credit repair and business funding to your tax practice, under your own brand. Watch the full demo." Headline: "See the platform in action." Button: Watch more.

**On-camera version (about 45 s), same link with `utm_content=camera`:**
> Tax pros. Your clients are about to get their refunds, and a lot of them are going to apply for business funding and get denied because of their credit. You already have their trust, their financials and their tax return. What you don't have is a way to fix the credit standing between them and that funding. So I built one, and it runs under your brand. Your client uploads their credit report. The platform audits all three bureaus and writes the dispute letters, in the right order, with the compliance paperwork built in. Your team never writes a letter. When their scores improve, they move into funding, and you share in it. I recorded the full demo so you can see exactly how it works. Tap the link and watch it.
