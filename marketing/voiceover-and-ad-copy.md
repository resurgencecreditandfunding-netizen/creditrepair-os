# Leverage IQ: voiceover scripts and ad copy

## How to record the voiceovers

- **Voice:** warm, confident, conversational. Like explaining something to a friend who runs a business, not like an announcer.
- **Pace:** steady, about 2.5 words a second. Each scene line below ends where the video cuts, so pause briefly at every `/`.
- **Say it like this:**
  - "Leverage IQ" is *LEV-er-ij eye-cue*.
  - "FCRA" is always said in full: "the Fair Credit Reporting Act."
  - "$147" is "a hundred and forty-seven dollars."
- **Takes:** record each numbered line as its own take with a second of silence on either side, so each line can be placed on its scene. A phone in a quiet, carpeted room is fine.
- **AI voice:** paste the "full read" block into ElevenLabs or HeyGen. Slow the speed one notch if a line runs past its scene.

## Why the copy is worded this way

- **Nothing about the reader's own credit.** Meta rejects ads that say or imply something about the viewer's finances, such as "your bad credit" or "Is your score holding you back?" Every line here talks about the activity ("doing credit repair") or the product.
- **No promised results.** There are no removals, point gains, approvals or "guaranteed." The landing page carries the full disclosures.
- **Price stated plainly.** Every consumer ad pairs "Free to start" with the $37, so nobody feels misled at checkout.
- **No earnings figures for operators.** "Earn a share" carries no dollar amount or percentage, because a specific number is an earnings claim the FTC treats strictly. Add a rate only once it's written into your operator agreement.
- **Special Ad Category.** Run the consumer ads under Meta's **Financial products and services** category.
